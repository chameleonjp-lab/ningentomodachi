## 実ロボ調査付録

### Actual hierarchy and coordinate conventions

基準commitの実GLBから階層・surface・source animationを読取測定。映像受入ではない。

- Four category GLBs share one compatible hierarchy: **53 bones**, **five skinned material primitives**, 47,198 triangles. The sampled surface contains **46,181 vertices** across those primitives. They are not five independent characters.
- Root hierarchy: `ANDROID_RIG` (node54, static translation `[0,-0.02199999988079071,0]`) → `root` (node52) → `pelvis` (node51).
- Torso chain: `pelvis` → `spine.01` → `spine.02` → `chest` → `neck` → `head`.
- Arms: `chest` → `clavicle.L/R` → `upper_arm.L/R` → `forearm.L/R` → `hand.L/R`, with finger/thumb descendants.
- Legs: `pelvis` → `thigh.L/R` → `shin.L/R` → `foot.L/R` → `toe.L/R`.
- Source dot names are sanitized by Three PropertyBinding: `spine.01` → `spine01`, `hand.L` → `handL`, `upper_arm.L` → `upper_armL`, etc. Bind authored tracks to the **loaded hierarchy's exact names**, or use explicit source-index→loaded-node mapping. Do not alternate naming conventions by guess.
- Actor/source basis: **+Y up, +Z forward, +X toward robot left**. Source Blender forward -Y is exported as glTF +Z. Right/left fighting directions use a rigid facing rotation; they are not skeletal left/right mirroring.
- Idle0 joint centers (actor-local metres): pelvisY1.083; spine01Y1.208; spine02Y1.378; chestY1.548; neckY1.698; head originY1.808. Head origin is not head top. Hip centers X±.155, Y1.083. Knee centers X±.173,Y.593; ankles X±.174,Y.146; toe origins Y.053,Z.139.
- Bone lengths: thigh→knee .490660846 m; shin→ankle .447252959 m; upper-arm→elbow .341322422 m; elbow→wrist .293318897 m. Preserve the rig and bind pose; the adopted .95 m pelvis/foot-width sketches are authoring guides, not a license to rescale these bones.

### Axis signs verified numerically, not inferred from names

The Idle torso bones have identity actor-space bases. Leg local +Y points down and local +Z approximately backward; both leg local +X point approximately actor +X. At Idle0, post-multiplying a local +X rotation of +.01 rad produces:

| Edited bone | Observed endpoint | Actor-local endpoint delta Z |
|---|---|---:|
| pelvis | neck | +.006150 m (forward) |
| spine01 | neck | +.004900 m (forward) |
| thighL / thighR | knee | -.004901 m (backward) |
| shinL / shinR | ankle | -.004469 m (backward) |

Thus a knee-forward crouch cannot use the same positive-X offset on torso, thigh, and shin. Use per-bone bind/reference-aware quaternion deltas or a qualified two-bone solve with explicit knee poles and sole orientation. Both legs require independent checks; mirrored Y/Z offsets must not be blindly reused. These probes establish the local neighborhood only, not a universal Euler law under arbitrary torso yaw.
