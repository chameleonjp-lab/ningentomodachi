# Two grounded punch diagnostics, not an accepted damage catalog

This immutable source-derived batch contains PunchCombo4's four original events
and PalmStrike's one source peak. It does not activate a production registry,
change original source bytes, imply full-roster completion, or claim exact mesh
contact. All acceptance and gameplay-allowance fields remain null.

## Source and timing

The built-in original speed 1.5 punch GLB and manifest pins are in
`tools/assets/groundedPunchDiagnostic.ts`; these already-retimed clips play at
runtime speed 1. Preserve exact FLOAT accessor times, source first-key hold,
original clip identities and source root curves.

PunchCombo4 preserves L F11–12 / peak 12, R F23–24 / peak 24,
L F38–40 / peak 39, and R F58–60 / peak 59. Its first two peaks occur at the
inclusive source interval ends. The developer inclusive-accessor-cell policy
maps each inclusive interval to a half-open interval ending at the next source
accessor frame, rather than excluding those peaks.

PalmStrike's source provides only right-hand peak 25. F24–26 is a newly authored,
provisional diagnostic interval, not a source-authored or approved runtime
window. It must not be widened to obtain a hit.

## Anatomy and geometry

Original primitive, triangle, vertex, joint and barycentric identities are
retained. Source bone-bind coordinates and original unnormalized transformed
normals define the explicit source-only clipping predicates. No welded points,
dominant-weight skin approximation, forced facing or target-dependent fit is
used. The recipe describes every predicate.

The combo region is a distal-hand/proximal-finger-rim proposal, not a claim that
the posed source is a closed fist. Its strict original-corner rim-restoration
rule restores 64 left and 68 right faces; this is not the prior Uppercut 76-face
selection or its acceptance. Palm's proximal-palmar-heel proposal must be
inspected against the actual hand pose. If the interpretation fails, retain the
point/region evidence separately instead of presenting an accepted palm shape.

One deterministic source-reference AABB-midpoint sphere was fitted per event.
The reference is the original mesh-bind coordinate frame recovered through the
hand skin-affine transform, not an invented anatomical joint frame. All original
active keys plus a 240 Hz finite grid contribute corners. The 1e-7 m CPU arithmetic
guard is not an interpolation or gameplay allowance.

## Known limitations retained

The raw serialized spheres have finite quarter-key escapes: combo hook about
0.026538 mm, combo uppercut 0.008202 mm and Palm 0.001107 mm. Jab/cross finite probes
being contained is not a continuous-containment certificate. Directional excess
reaches 37.20 mm on the combo uppercut and 32.17 mm on Palm. No numerical padding,
refit, target clearance allowance, Uppercut 20/35 mm trial or HP acceptance is
transferred to this batch.

The separate numerical qualification target is at most 0.25 mm additional
interpolation enclosure with a separate 1e-7 m CPU arithmetic guard. No such
certificate or padding is applied here. Original-source equivalence retains
its 1e-10 m criterion; no GPU or formal rounded-arithmetic proof is claimed.

The explicit grounded-posture-Y diagnostic policy retains source posture Y in
visual and strike geometry, emits transport Y=0 and leaves vertical ownership to
spatial jump/gravity. Raw Y remains in provenance. The finite source qualification covers 287 poses and 13,253,947 original
vertices through the public factory, with maximum source-equivalence error below
7.2e-15 m under unchanged translated/rotated parents. The original whole-clip
floor minima, approximately -0.683 mm for PunchCombo4 and -0.065 mm for Palm,
remain unchanged. This is not continuous support proof or actual C composition.
Authored floor deviations must not be silently normalized.

Depth-tested source overlays show partially curled combo hands and an open
PalmStrike hand. The lower-palmar proposal is visible, but these observations do
not accept contact anatomy, the provisional Palm interval or a damage proxy.
The overlays use diagnostic colors and fixed low-sample CPU lighting, not final
material or device-render acceptance.

## Test-only construction

Read the original source/manifest and these two fixture byte arrays, then call
`loadGroundedPunchDiagnostics`. It verifies built-in source, manifest, candidate
and recipe hashes without a caller trust override, and returns immutable
existing `MotionAsset` data, explicit `ClipRootPolicy`, source descriptors,
original polygon identities and finite measurements. Every returned item says
`diagnostic-broadphase-only` with null acceptance and allowance.

Pass the explicit policies to the pinned public visual factory and check
`assertRootPolicyCompatibility` for the same assets before preparing a test
catalog. A owns actual Core/C-facing, event-time contact, gravity and near-miss
qualification. Proxy candidates, intended hand contact and visible target
contact are distinct observations. No synthetic timing-only registry can serve
as this batch's source evidence.
